What pump.fun does not have

There is no "charity" field on pump.fun. No checkbox, no partner list, no built-in way to attach a nonprofit to a coin. The only thing a coin has is a creator, and the only thing a creator has is a fee vault. So the pairing is not a feature we found — it is the one we build on top: the vault, and a standing instruction to empty it into a donation route that belongs to somebody else.

    06 — WHAT WE ARE NOT GOING TO PRETEND

    The uncomfortable parts, in the same size type.

    "Verified" means registered, not partnered.

    The tick means we matched the organisation to a real registered nonprofit and a working donation route. It does not mean they endorse this, know about this, or have agreed to anything. We do not use their logos, because a logo next to a launch button would claim a relationship that does not exist.

    A coin is not a donation.

    You are buying a memecoin. It can go to zero and it probably will. The donation is funded by trading fees, not by your purchase, and nothing you buy gives you a tax receipt for anything.

    A quiet coin donates nothing.

    No trading, no fee, no donation. A coin nobody touches sends a charity exactly zero, and its row will say so rather than sitting blank and hopeful.

    The rail is not ours.

    Donation processors have minimums, jurisdictions and rules, and they can change them without asking us. If a route closes, coins depending on it cannot donate, and that appears on the board as a stopped row with the reason.

    Charities are not a shield.

    Pointing a fee at a good cause does not make a memecoin a good investment, and we are not going to write a sentence that implies it does.

    The pad's program is not live.

    Launching from here opens pump.fun with your coin's details ready. The fee-routing instruction is the pad's job, and until its program ships that part is on you — so every charity's own donation page is one click from its row.
